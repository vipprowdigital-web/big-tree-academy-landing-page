# Surface: landing page (frontend/src/app/page.jsx)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a beginner (17-25) with family, secondarily a career changer, comparing academies on a phone. Action: enquire, call, WhatsApp, or request a course seat.
Proof on hand: real studio and class photos, student work gallery, testimonials, courses, articles (all from the API). No invented outcomes.
Pinned by the user: white background; primary plum for navbar, footer and actions; secondary cream only as a sparing accent; modern, not fancy type; headings not too big; little text, more images and graphics; looks like an academy; professional and sophisticated; articles section; copyright centered on mobile.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A beauty-academy prospectus run as a lookbook: real class and student photography carries the page, while institutional structure (programmes, how you learn, certification, admission steps) reads like an academy, not a salon brochure. Refuses the huge-serif editorial salon page and the card-grid-of-icons template.

OWN-WORLD: White ground. Plum #28082e for the fixed navbar, every primary action, the testimonial band and the footer. Cream #ffe9cb as a small accent only (image mats, badges, the active filter). Faint plum-tinted off-white bands for rhythm. One modern sans (Manrope), headings 700 at moderate size. 16px image radius, 12px control radius, one hairline plum-tint border for elevation, no shadows. Photography is the decoration; the logo's terracotta stays in the logo.

STORY: In seconds the visitor sees a real studio class, understands hands-on practice, expert mentors and certification, browses programmes with price and duration, trusts the student work and testimonials, skims articles, and enquires through the form, WhatsApp or phone.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim contact strip and plum navbar. Left half: headline about 3.4rem on desktop, one supporting sentence, a plum "Explore programmes" button and a quiet "Enquire now" link, then three icon facts (hands-on practice, expert mentors, certification). Right half: a three-photo collage from real class photos (large class demo, two smaller portrait crops) with a small cream badge about certification on completion.

FORM: Student lookbook / academy prospectus, candidate 4 of the grounded list (prospectus spread, faculty portal, studio floor plan, lookbook, diploma document, shade chart, timetable board); seed: da308b283f37d143

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
